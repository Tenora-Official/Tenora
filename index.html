Size guide

Timeless pieces for every generation. All measurements are body measurements in centimetres (cm).

Between two sizes? Choose the larger size for more growing room.

How to measure
  • Measure the body, not an existing garment.
  • Height — stand straight without shoes and measure from the top of the head to the floor.
  • Chest / Bust — measure around the fullest part of the chest or bust, keeping the tape level and comfortable.
  • Waist — measure around the natural waist without pulling the tape tight.
  • Hip — measure around the fullest part of the hips / seat.
  • For babies and children, use height as the first reference. For women and men, compare bust / chest, waist and hip with the chart.
TENORA fit guide
  • Slim — closer to the body with less ease. Fitted tops, dresses, structured styles.
  • Regular — balanced everyday ease. T-shirts, shirts, everyday dresses.
  • Relaxed — more room for movement and comfort. Oversized tops, sweatshirts, loungewear, selected babywear.

These charts are TENORA's starting size standards. Finished garment measurements vary by style, fabric and intended fit, so always check the product page for Fit: Slim, Regular or Relaxed. For children, age ranges are approximate; height and body measurements are the better guide.
